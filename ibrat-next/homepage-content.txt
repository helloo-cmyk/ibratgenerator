## 12. Why Your Brat Image Looks Wrong
H2: Why Your Brat Image Looks Wrong
- Wrong Green Color: Most tools use #c1ff00 or lime green which completely changes the vibe. The real brat green is #89CC04 - darker, slightly more olive, and much more grounded. This tool uses the correct hex code automatically so you never have to guess.
- Text Too Sharp: The original album cover has slightly blurred text - not crisp, perfect digital type. Using standard sharp fonts ruins the effect. Use the Softness slider (1-1.5px) for an authentic, slightly messy look.
- Too Many Words: Brat text works with 1 to 4 words maximum. More than that and the aesthetic completely breaks down because the typography loses its visual weight. Short and punchy always beats long and descriptive sentences.
- Wrong Aspect Ratio: Make sure to use Square (1:1) for profile pictures so it fits the crop perfectly. Choose 9:16 for TikTok and Instagram stories to fill the whole screen. Use 16:9 for Twitter headers and YouTube thumbnails. This generator has all four presets built right in.
